Note: the perception indicators come from the microdata of the Bogotá Cómo Vamos 2025 Citizen Perception Survey, filtered to young people aged 18 to 25, an unweighted subsample of 225 cases. That subsample was not designed to be statistically representative on its own, so the percentages should be read as indicative of the views of the young people surveyed.

The environmental culture indicators come from the Environmental Culture module of the Biennial Culture Survey 2025 (young people aged 13 to 28), weighted with the survey expansion factor. The two surveys cover different populations and age ranges, so their results are not comparable. The full technical sheets are published by Bogotá Cómo Vamos and the Secretariat of Culture, Recreation and Sport.
