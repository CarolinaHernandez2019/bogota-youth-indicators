The labour market situation of young people in Bogotá, based on DANE’s Integrated Household Survey (GEIH).

Results of the Biennial Culture Survey 2025 and the Sports Practice and Quality of Life Survey 2024 on cultural, recreational and sports activities for young people.

Results of the Bogotá Cómo Vamos 2025 Citizen Perception Survey and the Biennial Culture Survey 2025 (Environmental Culture module) on habitat.
